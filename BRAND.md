## Positioning statement

> For South Africa's licensed cannabis and hemp cultivators, KhulaGrow is the
> farm management system that turns a SAHPRA inspection from a scramble into
> a formality — built for the field first, priced so a small grower can
> actually afford it, and made by people who understand that load-shedding
> doesn't care about your compliance deadline.

## Brand pillars

- **Audit-ready, always** — not a feature, the whole product's reason to exist.
- **Field-first** — designed for muddy boots and patchy signal, not an office desk.
- **Plainly priced** — R1,500/month, unlimited users, no quote calls. Rare in
  this category and worth repeating everywhere.
- **Locally built** — a South African product for a South African regulator,
  not a US platform with the currency changed.

## Voice, in practice

- Say "SAHPRA inspection," "batch," "site" — the words a farm manager already
  uses — not "workflow" or "solution."
- Confidence without jargon. *"Muddy boots, not office desks"* is the best
  line on the site — protect it, and write more like it.
- Never talk down about paper records. Most prospects are using them right
  now. Respect, then replace.
- Lead with the inspection, not the software. Nobody wakes up wanting
  compliance software; they wake up dreading the audit.
- No hype words: revolutionize, seamless, game-changing, AI-powered, disrupt,
  ecosystem, robust, at scale, effortless. None of these appear on the site
  today — keep it that way.
- Claims should be checkable, not aspirational. *"3 taps in the tunnel,
  gloves on, no signal"* beats *"easy to use"* every time.

## How the voice flexes by channel

Same voice, different register:

| Channel | Register |
|---|---|
| Homepage / marketing | Warmest end of the range. Sells outcomes, stays specific and checkable. |
| Resource guides (`/resources`) | Teacherly, plain, zero sales language. States uncertainty rather than bluffing — the `<Verify>` markers in draft guides are this principle, encoded. |
| Regulator correspondence (SAHPRA, DALRRD) | Formal, precise, explicit disclaimers, no sales register at all. |
| LinkedIn (founder-led) | First-person voice. Field-story posts can be anecdotal; regulatory-insight posts read like the guides, not like ads. |
| Support / in-app | Warm, short sentences, no jargon beyond what the reader already uses daily. |
